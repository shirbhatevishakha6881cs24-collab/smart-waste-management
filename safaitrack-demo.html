Why this exists

Overflowing bins on roads, campuses and parks usually stay unreported because reporting is slow, or a report goes nowhere once filed.

  • One shared list. Every report lands in the same public queue — nothing sits in a private inbox.
  • Age is visible. Reports older than 48 hours are flagged in red, on the list and in the admin queue, so backlog can't hide.
  • Upvotes surface urgency. If ten people confirm the same overflowing bin, it should outrank a one-off report.
  • Status is public. Reported → In Progress → Resolved, with a note from the crew, visible to whoever reported it.
